Getting started

Start a conversation with our school.

Admission availability, class placement and subject options depend on the applicant and current school year. Contact the school to confirm the latest process and requirements.

  1. 01

    Make an enquiry

    Ask the school about current availability, suitable class placement and the next steps.

  2. 02

    Discuss the right pathway

    Review the learner’s previous studies and ask about available courses and subject combinations.

  3. 03

    Confirm documents and dates

    Check which school records to bring and confirm current admission dates directly with the office.

Please note: Application deadlines, fees and entry requirements can change. Please confirm them directly with the school before applying.
